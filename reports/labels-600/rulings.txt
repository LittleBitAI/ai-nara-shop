Organizer rulings (official contest Q&A S7 and notice S6, restated without any example notice).
Where a ruling and your own reading of the statutes differ, follow the ruling.

General scope
- Judge from the whole record: the 공고문 and every attached document actually present in the notice
  (과업지시서, 제안요청서, 규격서 ...), not only the 입찰참가자격 section.
- A document that is only named or referenced (입찰유의서, 계약일반조건 ...) but whose text is not in the
  notice is not input.
- The mere presence of a word or a document name is not a participation requirement. Sanction notices
  and lists of documents to submit are not requirements to hold that status as a 참가자격.
- A 중소기업자 requirement stated only in the 과업지시서 still counts; its absence from the 공고문 alone does
  not make the restriction missing. v16 and v18 need different company sizes, so one sentence does not
  settle both.

v1 참가자격 특정기관 제한
- Not limited to naming an institution. Restricting participation, without a statutory basis, to holders
  of a specific facility or of personnel/equipment above a set scale is also in scope.
- Do not decide by whether the requirement is personnel or equipment, or whether a number is given.
  Weigh what the rules for that contract method allow together with the task need shown in the notice.
  A small 수의계약 is not automatically compliant, and a 제한경쟁 is not automatically compliant just
  because the restriction relates to the task.
- 지방자치단체 입찰 및 계약 집행기준 제5장 lets a local small 수의계약 quotation limit bidders by equipment
  or facilities; it does not apply to 국가계약법 contracts.

v2 고시금액 미만 실적제한
- A 실적 restriction when 추정가격 is below the 고시금액 is a violation. The exception allowing it below
  1억원 exists only for 지방계약 + 소액수의 견적.

v5-v7 지역제한 amounts (notice S6)
- 지방계약, 물품·일반용역: 시·도 발주 3억 5천만원; 세종특별자치시 and 시·군·구 발주 5억원.
- An anonymised region token `[지역:rN|단위=기초|...]` means a 시·군·구 level place name stands there;
  such a restriction is not a single 광역 restriction.

v9 과업지시서 특정 모델명 명시
- Holds when a specific model name or manufacturer name is written in the specifications. Price+spec
  simultaneous bidding, deduction-type evaluation, and a blank for the bidder to write its model name
  are not exclusions.
- "동등 이상" next to the name has no uniform rule; decide from the item definition and the whole
  document, not from that phrase alone.

v10-v18 중기간 경쟁제품 branches
- 경쟁제품 are designated by 세부품명, not by goods code. Identify the object from the notice, task and
  registration requirements and meta `조항호내용`, match it by name against the provided catalogue (a
  number is not required), then apply 특이사항.
- For 특이사항 amounts: a 추정가격 cap uses meta `입찰추정가격`; the 「소프트웨어 진흥법 제48조 적용」 type uses
  사업금액 = 추정가격 + 부가가치세 (지침 제3조①3; long-term maintenance and split orders follow 4호·5호).
  `배정예산금액` is not always the base.
- v10 (직접생산), v11 (중소기업 확인서) and v20 (SW 사업금액 bracket restriction wording) are judged each by
  its own definition; one amount bracket does not decide between them. The 20억원 in 별표 1 is a floor for
  large-company participation, not an applicability floor for v20.
- v10: whether requiring a 직접생산확인증명서 as a participation qualification is present.
- v13: in competitive bidding for a 경쟁제품, whether participation is limited to 소기업·소상공인
  (excluding 중기업). No amount bracket is required. Exception: small 수의 quotation. The 1억 미만
  소기업·소상공인 special case belongs to the general-product branch, not to 경쟁제품.
- v14-v18 "일반물품" is not goods only; it includes services that are not 경쟁제품.
- v16 and v18 are not limited to 제한경쟁; 일반경쟁 is not excluded by contract method alone.
- The 비고 "판로지원 예외 명시한 경우" (v15, v16, v18) means the exception reason is written directly in
  the notice or its attachments. Do not infer it from the service nature or from a 나라장터 category.

v24 공고서와 나라장터 입력값 상이
- The only axes are the four in the 비고: 예산, 계약방법, 지역제한, 업종. Comparing the nature of the
  restriction reasons in 조항호내용 is not an axis.
- The 예산 axis compares the notice's 사업예산 with the meta budget fields (`배정예산금액`, `입찰추정가격`).
- Typos are not violations; an obvious typo may be normalised when comparing.
